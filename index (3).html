    How to use the companion

    1. Type a real plant problem (yellow leaf, black spot, wilt).
    2. Pick indoor/outdoor, care vs story, and any genre.
    3. Click Grow companion note.
    4. Read care first, then the fiction pick — not a plant manual.
    5. Use My Plants, Reading List, and Journal to add your own items.
